HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS
01
🗑️
Bins Report Fill
Smart bins send real-time fill-level data to Mission Control. Every bin across Pathankot is monitored continuously.
02
🧠
AI Routes Trucks
The routing engine sorts bins by urgency and assigns the nearest truck using a greedy shortest-path algorithm — no wasted km.
03
♻️
Citizens Segregate
Residents separate General, Recyclable, Organic and Hazardous waste before disposal — each proper drop earns 2 Green Points.
04
🏆

Points unlock real rewards: grocery discounts at 500 pts, bus passes at 1000 pts, utility bill savings at 1500 pts.
